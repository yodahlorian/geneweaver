# Geneweaver

**Catch them. Merge them. Weave your own.**

[![Downloads](https://img.shields.io/github/downloads/yodahlorian/geneweaver/total?label=downloads&color=7fd16a)](../../releases/latest)

A creature-catching battler in a sunny world of tomorrow that went sideways. Nature grew back over the gadgets, and the wildlife
got tangled up in them. Catch creatures, battle in blind picks where speed shows you what your rival did NOT choose, and merge
your creatures into new ones nobody else has: every merge is decided by its parents, in order.

Beta by **Devious Developments**.

**Download:** [Releases -> Latest](../../releases/latest) (Windows, one .exe, no install).
**On iPhone and Android:** [geneweaver-beta](https://github.com/yodahlorian/geneweaver-beta) (the `android` release holds the .apk).

## What changed in 0.2.0

- A new opening: Dr. Hollis's lab, five pedestals, look at each creature before you choose your partner.
- Redrawn people (player, Dr. Hollis, trainers) with a proper walk.
- Creature fronts drawn at full size, no floating parts.

- Towns in full colour: three-quarter-view buildings, even doors, atomic-age roofs.
- Buildings carry emblems instead of names: caduceus (heal), flask (lab), money bag (shop), fist (gym), helix (Loom).

- The Loom shows the child's stat shape against both parents, its types, tier, cap and traits.
- A full stats screen: your own placed points in gold, trait bonuses in green and drawbacks in red, type matchups, lineage.
- Healing happens inside the Restore Center: the medic loads your cubes and the machine solves them back to health.
- New capture animation: the cube bursts on the creature, closes around it and solves itself in its colour.
- A new game emblem and app icon.

## What changed in 0.1.0

The first playable slice of Geneweaver.

- Pick your starter and set out across 7 towns, each with a healing centre, a shop, a gym and its own catch zone.
- 12 routes between the towns change every time you set foot on one: wild creatures, trainers or a market. Once you start a route, you finish it.
- Battles are blind picks: Attack, Block, Magic, Heal or Debuff. Every pick beats two and loses to two, and the faster creature gets a hint about what its rival did NOT choose.
- Five types, each strong against two and weak against two. Traits give every creature its own strengths and weaknesses.
- Catch creatures with the Capture Cube. Toss a food first to stop them running, keep them from fainting, or make them easier to catch. Lures bring the types you want.
- XP is shared by your whole team, and every level you place one stat point yourself.
- Merge two creatures at the Loom in Fusionville. The child looks like its parents, in order, so every merge is its own creature.
- The Shuttle Pad keeps a record of every creature you have met.
- 35 gym badges: 7 gyms, each beaten 5 times as they grow stronger.
- Chiptune music and sound, saves, and a handheld-console look with 6 styles in Options.
- Play with keyboard, gamepad, mouse or touch. On phones the console stands upright.
